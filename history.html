The History of Halloween

The history of this popular, fun event will be listed (as accurately as possible) chronologically :

  • ~ 3000 Years Ago : Here in Western Europe, the Celtic people practiced a festival known as Samhain (pronounced sow-in) on the night of October 31st
  • They believed the boundary between the living and the dead blurred on this night and that ghosts could return to earth on this day!
  • 43 A.D : Around this time the Roman Empire has conquered most of the Celtic territory (which would be under Roman rule for 400 years), two roman festivals would be combined with Samhain
  • ~9th Century : By this point of time, many Roman traditions and festivals had combined with celtic rites; in fact, in 1000 AD the Catholic Church created All Souls' Day on November 2nd (many believe this festival was created to replace the celtic tradition)

How did we get the event title "Halloween"?

Well, All Souls' Day was also called All-hallows/All-hallowmas and Samhain also began to be called All-Hallows Eve and hence now Halloween!
